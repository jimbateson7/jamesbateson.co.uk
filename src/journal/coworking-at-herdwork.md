R﻿ecently I had a look around and trialed a few hours working in the [Herdwork coworking space](https://underleyestate.com/work/herdwork/) at the Underley Estate in Kirkby Lonsdale. Yesterday I spent my first day working there, something I'm looking to do twice a week going forward.

## But you haven't even got a job, Jim

T﻿his is true, but I just wanted to write about the benefits and reasons I'll be trying to do this a couple of times a week, to remind myself to keep positive despite tough times.

### R﻿outine

T﻿he first benefit is that it keeps me in a routine. When searching for a job it can be easy to fall into a bad patterns, so having somewhere I can go and do some focused job searching, writing and personal development.

H﻿aving worked almost fully remote in my previous role, I'm keen for my next one to involve some time in an office space with colleagues, so getting up and ready and coming into an office style environment is great for me to adjust to that routine as well.

### T﻿he walk in

One of the things I'm enjoying about having moved back with my parents to Kirkby, is how much stunning countryside there is around. This is great for me getting back into my walking and running, and even the 2km ish walk to Herdwork is beautiful.

I﻿ found it such a nice start and end to the day. Time to think and appreciate the day as well as some fresh air and excercise away from the screen.
