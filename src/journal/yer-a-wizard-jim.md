Last week I visited the Harry Potter studio tour! It's something I have had on my bucket list for a while and lived up to expectations. But most importantly, with the house and job stress, such. Exactly the distraction and boost I needed.

The idea was suggested many months ago, and I'm so glad we saw it through.

One thing I took from the trip and that I am thankful for is that with looking for job and then having the double whammy of having to look for a new place to live, is that it doesn't matter.

I still have these problems and worries after the weekend away. But for a few days I forgot about this, I went back to my childhood self, embraced and enjoyed the experience.

I﻿ stayed in a lovely Airbnb, had a trip into London to experience one of their three Dishoom restaurants,  and generally just had a lovely time with some good friends.
