With a background in design, I'm now focused on the front-end and especially enjoy programming with HTML and CSS and advocating for accessibility.

After discovering my passion for design and development at school when building a website for my five-a-side football team, I have over 10 years of experience in creating performant, accessible user experiences and have worked with clients such as Lakeland, Warburtons, and Hillarys.

I'm very much front-of-the-font-end and whilst have worked with and appreciate frameworks such as React and Vue, I enjoy championing semantic, accessible and low JavaScript (where possible) solutions. Advocating for progressive enhancement and doing the right thing for the user.

## It’s not all about the codes

Outside of work, I like to try and keep active, I am a keen trail/fell runner, and have run half-marathons, marathons and ultramarathons.

As well as running, I also enjoy getting my walking boots on and going for a hike, especially up in the Lake District fells, occasionally getting the bike out and also going to the gym.

When not getting outdoors, I'll be in the kitchen trying with varying degrees of success to rustle up some good food.
